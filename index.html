Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a ticket?

Search your route, choose a fare, enter passenger details exactly as shown on the passport, and complete payment.

When will I receive my ticket?

You will receive a booking confirmation by email after payment. Timing depends on the airline.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Changes and refunds follow the airline's fare rules. Contact us as early as possible and we will help.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Payment options will be listed at checkout. Contact us if you need help paying.

Is my personal information safe?

We only use your details to complete your booking. See our Privacy Policy for more.
