Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Airline ticket flexibility, cancellation penalties, and refund eligibility are governed by the specific airline fare rules attached to your ticket. Those fare conditions are displayed clearly before you complete checkout on get2fly.com. If you have questions about a fare before booking, contact our support team first.

1. Refundable and Non-Refundable Fares

  • Refundable fares: May be cancelled prior to departure in accordance with the airline’s fare conditions. Your refund will equal the ticket amount paid minus any applicable airline cancellation penalty and non-refundable service fees.
  • Non-refundable fares: Promotional, Economy Light, and Saver tickets are generally non-refundable once issued. Where permitted by the operating airline and local aviation rules, unused refundable government taxes or airport charges may be claimed upon request.
  • Partially used tickets: If you cancel a return or multi-segment journey after flying the outbound leg, the airline recalculates the flown segment at the applicable one-way fare, which often leaves little or no residual refund value.

2. How to Request a Cancellation, Refund, or Change

To submit a cancellation, refund, or itinerary change request, contact us as early as possible before your scheduled departure:

  • Email: support@get2fly.com
  • WhatsApp: +973 3757 6600

Please include your Get2Fly booking reference, airline PNR, and full passenger names. Airlines enforce strict cut-off deadlines prior to departure, and change or cancellation penalties often increase closer to travel.

3. Service Fees and Airline Penalties

  • Get2Fly booking service fee: Currently $0 (or the exact amount clearly itemized at checkout prior to payment). Once an airline ticket is issued, any applicable Get2Fly service fee is non-refundable, except where required by law or if Get2Fly made an error in issuing the ticket.
  • Voluntary changes and cancellations: Any applicable Get2Fly administrative handling fee is disclosed to you for approval before we process a voluntary change or cancellation, in addition to the airline’s own penalty and any fare difference.

4. Flight Date, Route, and Name Changes

  • Date and itinerary changes: Allowed only if permitted by the airline’s fare rules. You are responsible for the airline’s change fee, any fare or tax difference between the original and new flights, and any disclosed handling fee.
  • Passenger name corrections: Most airlines prohibit full passenger name transfers to another person. Minor spelling corrections are subject to the operating airline’s policy and fees, and may require cancelling and rebooking at current fares.

5. Missed Flights and No-Shows

If you fail to check in on time or do not board your flight (“no-show”) without cancelling in advance according to the airline’s deadline, airlines typically void the remaining itinerary—including connecting and return flights—with zero refund value, except for refundable airport taxes where permitted.

6. Airline Schedule Changes and Flight Cancellations

If an airline cancels your flight or makes a significant schedule change, we will notify you promptly using the contact details on your booking and assist you with the options offered by the airline—typically rebooking on an alternative flight at no extra cost or requesting an involuntary full refund under the airline’s policy and applicable passenger rights regulations.

7. Refund Processing Timeline and Payment Method

  • Processing timeline: Once we submit your approved refund request and receive confirmation/funds back from the airline, refunds typically take 3 to 5 business days to be processed back to your original payment method. (In rare cases involving complex interline or multi-airline tickets, airline authorization may take longer.)
  • Payment method and currency: All refunds are credited strictly to the original card or payment method used at checkout via Stripe. Your bank or card issuer may take an additional few days to post the credit, and exchange-rate fluctuations between booking and refund dates are determined by your card issuer.

8. Booking Errors and Payment Disputes

If a ticketing error occurs due to a mistake by Get2Fly LLC, we will correct the reservation at our expense or provide a full refund. If you experience any issue with your booking or refund, please contact us directly at support@get2fly.com before initiating a bank chargeback so we can resolve your request quickly.
