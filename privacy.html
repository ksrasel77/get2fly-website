Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Get2Fly LLC (“Get2Fly”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit get2fly.com (the “Site”) or book international flights across the Middle East, Asia, Europe, and worldwide destinations.

1. Information We Collect

To search flight availability, issue airline tickets, and support your trip, we collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact and booking details: Full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and billing country.
  • Passenger and travel document details: First and last names exactly as shown on each traveler’s passport, date of birth, gender, nationality, passport number, passport expiry date, and issuing country when required by operating airlines or immigration authorities.
  • Payment and transaction records: Transaction ID, payment status, billing details, and partial card metadata. Card payments are processed securely by Stripe; Get2Fly does not store full credit or debit card numbers on its servers.
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, browser type, device information, approximate region/currency preferences, and pages visited on the Site.
  • Customer support communications: Emails, WhatsApp messages, and booking inquiries you send to our support team.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Convenience features such as recent flight searches, saved routes, preferred currencies, and traveler shortcuts are currently stored locally in your web browser storage on your device. Clearing your browser storage or site data removes these locally saved preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Search live airline fares, create flight reservations, issue electronic tickets (e-tickets), and deliver booking confirmations.
  • Communicate itinerary updates, airline schedule changes, check-in reminders, and customer support responses.
  • Process payments, verify transactions, prevent fraud, and comply with aviation security and anti-fraud rules.
  • Send essential transactional and service messages related to your booking. We do not send unsolicited promotional emails unless you explicitly opt in.
  • Maintain Site reliability, diagnose technical issues, and improve route search performance.
  • Comply with applicable tax, accounting, aviation, and legal obligations.

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We share personal information only as strictly necessary to fulfill your travel booking and operate our service:

  • Operating airlines and travel suppliers: Required to reserve seats, issue tickets, process changes or refunds, and facilitate airport check-in.
  • Ticketing and distribution infrastructure partners: Including our airline distribution partner Duffel, which transmits booking and passenger details to airlines.
  • Payment processors: Stripe processes payments, fraud screening, and refunds securely under PCI-DSS standards.
  • Essential technology providers: Secure cloud hosting, transactional email delivery, and website performance tools operating under confidentiality obligations.
  • Government and aviation authorities: Customs, border control, and regulatory agencies when required by law or mandatory passenger information rules (such as API/PNR requirements).

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

5. International Data Transfers

Because international air travel connects airlines, airports, and technology providers across the United States, the Middle East, Asia, Europe, and other regions, your booking information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence. We take appropriate contractual and technical safeguards to protect your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

6. Data Retention

We retain booking, passenger, and accounting records for up to 3 years following your travel date to provide post-booking support, handle airline refund or dispute inquiries, and meet legal, tax, and financial record-keeping obligations. After this period, personal records are securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Data Security

We protect your information using HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, PCI-compliant payment processing via Stripe, restricted administrative access, and secure infrastructure controls. While no internet transmission is 100% immune to risk, we continuously maintain safeguards to protect your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR/UK GDPR or applicable regional privacy laws), you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal data, or object to certain processing activities. To exercise your rights, email support@get2fly.com. We may verify your identity before fulfilling requests and may retain limited records where legally required.

9. Cookies and Essential Storage

Get2Fly uses strictly necessary cookies and browser storage to keep your flight search session, currency selection, and checkout flow working properly. Essential cookies cannot be switched off without affecting Site functionality. You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Site is intended for use by adults aged 18 and older. Parents or legal guardians may enter passenger information for children and infants traveling on a booking. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from minors without adult involvement.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when the latest version took effect.
